## 🎯 What You're Building

A **read-only, observation-only** Laravel add-on that:
- Tracks trader positions across 5 DEX platforms
- Computes verified PnL, funding, and liquidation metrics
- Generates AI-driven behavioral insights
- Provides admin analytics dashboard with leaderboards

**Key Constraints** (Enforced):
- ❌ No trade execution
- ❌ No wallet management
- ❌ No mock data
- ❌ User-facing routes
